# Privacy Policy — Transalp Bordcomputer Companion-App

**Last updated:** 2026-10-20

## Overview

The Transalp Bordcomputer companion app ("the App") connects to your Transalp
Bordcomputer device via Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE). This privacy policy describes
how data is handled.

## Data Collected

### Data from the Bordcomputer

The App receives the following data from the Bordcomputer over a local BLE
connection:

- **Telemetry:** Speed, RPM, gear, coolant temperature, ambient temperature,
  fuel level, vehicle voltage, GPS coordinates, altitude, heading, satellite
  count, HDOP
- **Navigation:** Next maneuver, route progress, road name, speed limit
- **CarPlay:** Connection status, active app, music metadata (artist, title, album)
- **Diagnostics:** DTC fault codes, sensor status, self-test results, system
  status (uptime, heap, battery, CPU temperature)
- **Configuration:** Bordcomputer settings (brightness, GPS rate, RPM redline,
  shift light, logging settings)

### Data Stored on the Device

- Tour history (date, distance, duration, max speed, max RPM) — stored locally
  on the phone, no cloud sync
- Controller button mapping — stored locally
- App settings — stored locally

### Data NOT Collected

The App does **not**:

- Collect, transmit, or sell personal data to third parties
- Use analytics or tracking SDKs
- Require an account or login
- Upload data to any cloud service
- Access contacts, photos, or other personal data on your phone

## Bluetooth Usage

The App uses Bluetooth Low Energy to communicate with the Bordcomputer.
Bluetooth permissions are requested at runtime. Bluetooth is only used while
the App is active and connected to the Bordcomputer.

## Data Sharing

No data is shared with third parties. All data remains on your device.

## Data Security

BLE communication with the Bordcomputer is encrypted (bonded connection).
No data is transmitted over the internet.

## Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13. No data is collected from anyone.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be posted
in the App and on this page.
